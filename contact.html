About ELARA

ELARA is a Smart Hospital Communication Platform designed to humanise technology in healthcare. We bridge the gap between patients and their care teams — making hospital stays more comfortable, informed, and connected.

From the moment you're admitted, ELARA gives you access to your medical records, upcoming appointments, educational content about your condition, entertainment to keep you comfortable, and a direct line to request help from nurses — all from one simple platform.

Our mission is to reduce patient anxiety, improve health literacy, and empower patients to be active participants in their own care — while helping hospitals run more efficiently.
